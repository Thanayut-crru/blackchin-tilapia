# Third-party data and redistribution status

This archive separates code licensing from data licensing.

## WorldClim 2.1

The ten environmental rasters are **not included**. WorldClim permits academic
use but states that redistribution is not allowed without prior permission.
`prepare_worldclim.py` downloads the original BIO and elevation archives from
the provider and reconstructs the fixed analysis grid.

- Source: https://www.worldclim.org/data/worldclim21.html
- Terms: https://www.worldclim.org/about.html
- Citation: Fick, S.E. & Hijmans, R.J. (2017), International Journal of Climatology 37:4302-4315.

## OpenStreetMap / Geofabrik-derived layers

`A1_dist_waterway.tif`, `A2_waterway_order.tif`, `P2_dist_road.tif`,
`P3_road_density.tif`, `P4_dist_urban.tif`, and the aquatic display mask were
derived from OpenStreetMap data obtained through Geofabrik. They are supplied
with attribution to OpenStreetMap contributors and remain subject to ODbL 1.0.
The study snapshot date recorded by the manuscript is 12 June 2026.

- Copyright and licence: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- ODbL 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

## WorldPop

`P1_pop_density.tif` is a model-grid derivative of the Thailand 2020 WorldPop
UN-adjusted population raster. Attribute WorldPop/University of Southampton and
retain the source dataset's licence and citation.

- Source: https://www.worldpop.org/
- Data portal: https://data.worldpop.org/

## GBIF / iNaturalist occurrence records

The modelling table contains stable source identifiers where available. GBIF-
mediated datasets can carry CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-NC licences at the dataset
level. Before Zenodo publication, insert the exact GBIF download DOI and licence
statement in `PRE_UPLOAD_CHECKLIST.txt`, the manuscript, and Zenodo metadata.
Do not replace source-specific terms with the repository MIT licence.

- GBIF terms: https://www.gbif.org/terms

## GADM

GADM country and province boundaries are not included. If regenerating the AOA
and province summaries, obtain GADM 4.1 directly and place files at:

- `data/thailand_boundary.geojson`
- `data/thailand_provinces.geojson`

Review https://gadm.org/license.html before use or redistribution.
